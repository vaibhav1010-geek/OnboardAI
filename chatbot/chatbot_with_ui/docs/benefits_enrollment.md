# Benefits Enrollment Guide
Last updated: 2026-03-12
Applies to: All employees
Owner: People Team

## Health insurance
Every employee is covered by the group health insurance plan with a cover of Rs 5 lakh. The plan covers the employee, a spouse or partner, and up to 2 children.

## Enrollment deadline
You must enroll in BenefitsHub within 30 days of your joining date. If you miss the deadline, you can only enroll during the annual window in November, so do not delay.

## How to enroll
1. Log in to BenefitsHub with your company email.
2. Choose Health Insurance and add your dependents.
3. Upload the required documents: ID proof for each dependent and a marriage certificate or birth certificates where relevant.
4. Submit. You receive a confirmation email within 3 working days.

## Provident fund
Provident fund contributions start from your first payroll. You can choose your contribution percentage in BenefitsHub during your first month.

## Questions
For benefit questions, message #ask-hr or email people@acmedemo.example. For claim issues, contact the insurance helpdesk number printed on your insurance card.
