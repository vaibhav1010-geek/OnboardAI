# Expense Reimbursement Policy
Last updated: 2026-02-10
Applies to: All employees
Owner: Finance Team

## What you can claim
Business travel, client meals, approved software, and work-related equipment. Personal expenses and fines are never reimbursable.

## Limits
- Meals while travelling on business: up to Rs 800 per meal.
- Receipts are required for any single expense above Rs 500.
- Any single expense above Rs 10,000 needs prior written approval from your manager.

## How to submit
Submit claims in the ExpenseDesk portal within 30 days of the expense date. Claims submitted after 30 days are rejected unless Finance approves an exception.

## Payment
Approved claims are paid in the next payroll cycle, and always within 10 working days of approval. Questions go to finance@acmedemo.example.
