# Expense Reimbursement Policy (2023 version)
Last updated: 2023-04-01
Applies to: All employees
Owner: Finance Team

This is the older version of the expense policy. It has been replaced, but this copy was never removed from the shared drive.

## Limits
- Meals while travelling on business: up to Rs 600 per meal.
- Receipts are required for any single expense above Rs 1,000.

## How to submit
Submit claims by email to finance@acmedemo.example within 45 days of the expense date.

## Payment
Approved claims are paid within 15 working days of approval.
