# First Week Checklist
Last updated: 2026-02-25
Applies to: All employees
Owner: People Team

## Day 1 (everyone)
- Collect your laptop and sign in to company email.
- Join Slack and introduce yourself in #new-joiners.
- Set up 1Password and multi-factor authentication.
- Meet your manager and your onboarding buddy.

## Days 2 to 3
- Complete HR paperwork in PeopleHub within 3 working days.
- Raise access requests for the tools you need (see the Tool Access Matrix).

## Role-specific tools
- Engineering: GitHub, Jira, and AWS Console (AWS only after security training).
- Sales: Salesforce.
- Design: Figma and Jira.

## By the end of week 1
- Complete the security awareness training (due within 7 days of joining).
- Hold a first one-to-one with your manager to agree your 30-60-90 day goals.

## By day 30
- Enroll in health insurance in BenefitsHub.
- Submit any first expense claims.
