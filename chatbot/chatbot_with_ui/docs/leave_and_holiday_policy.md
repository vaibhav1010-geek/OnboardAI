# Leave and Holiday Policy
Last updated: 2026-01-15
Applies to: All employees
Owner: People Team

## Annual leave
Every full-time employee gets 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at 2 days per month, starting from the joining date, and can be used from day one once it has accrued.

Up to 10 unused days can be carried over into the next year. Anything above 10 days lapses on 31 December.

## How to apply
Apply in the PeopleHub portal under Time Off > New Request. Leave of 3 or more consecutive working days must be requested at least 5 working days in advance. Your manager approves or declines within 2 working days.

## Sick leave
Employees get 8 paid sick days per year. A medical certificate is required when sick leave covers 3 or more consecutive days. Tell your manager as early as possible on the first day of absence.

## Public holidays
The company observes 10 public holidays per year. The list for each year is published in PeopleHub in December of the previous year. If you work in a different city, the holiday list for your office applies.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers get 26 weeks of paid parental leave and secondary caregivers get 4 weeks. Contact the People Team at people@acmedemo.example to start the process. Do not discuss personal medical details in chat; the People Team handles those privately.
