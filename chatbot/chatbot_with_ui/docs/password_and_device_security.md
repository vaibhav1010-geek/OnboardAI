# Password and Device Security Policy
Last updated: 2026-01-30
Applies to: All employees
Owner: Security Team

## Passwords
- Use the 1Password manager for every work account. Do not store passwords in notes, spreadsheets, or chat.
- Passwords must be at least 14 characters. Let 1Password generate them.
- Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all company accounts.

## Laptops and devices
- Disk encryption must stay on. It is enabled by IT on your laptop.
- Screens must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.
- Do not plug in personal USB drives. Use approved cloud storage to move files.
- Install operating system updates within 7 days of release.

## Lost or stolen devices
Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone within 1 hour to security@acmedemo.example, and also tell your manager. Do not wait to look for it first.

## Security training
All new employees must complete the security awareness training within 7 days of joining.
