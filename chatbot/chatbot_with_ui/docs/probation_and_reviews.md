# Probation and Performance Reviews
Last updated: 2026-01-20
Applies to: All employees
Owner: People Team

## Probation
New employees serve a probation period of 6 months. Leave accrues as normal during probation. The notice period during probation is 15 days on either side.

## Check-ins
Your manager holds formal check-ins at 3 months and at 6 months. At the 6-month check-in you receive a written confirmation letter if you are confirmed.

## After probation
The notice period becomes 60 days. Performance reviews run twice a year, in June and December, and use the goals agreed with your manager.
