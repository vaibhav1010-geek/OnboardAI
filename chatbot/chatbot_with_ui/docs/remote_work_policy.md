# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy
Last updated: 2026-02-01
Applies to: All employees
Owner: People Team

## Hybrid schedule
Employees work from the office 3 days a week: Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. Mondays and Fridays are flexible remote days unless your team says otherwise.

## Core hours
Everyone should be reachable between 11:00 and 16:00 IST on working days, even on remote days.

## Working from another city
You may work from a different city or country for up to 15 days per calendar year with your manager's written approval. Working abroad may have tax and legal implications, so tell the People Team before you travel.

## Home office allowance
After completing 90 days with the company, you can claim a one-time home office allowance of Rs 15,000 for a chair, desk or monitor. Submit it through ExpenseDesk with receipts.

## Internet
The company does not reimburse home internet, but a monthly mobile data allowance is available for on-call roles only.
