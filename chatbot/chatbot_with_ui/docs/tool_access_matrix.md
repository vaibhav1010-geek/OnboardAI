# Tool Access Matrix
Last updated: 2026-02-20
Applies to: All employees
Owner: IT Team

## Provisioned automatically on day 1
Company email, Slack, Google Workspace, and the 1Password password manager. You do not need to request these.

## Tools you must request
| Tool | Who can request | Approver | Typical turnaround |
|---|---|---|---|
| GitHub | Engineering | Engineering manager | 1 business day |
| AWS Console | Engineering | Engineering manager and Security team | 3 business days |
| Jira | Engineering, Design | Team lead | 1 business day |
| Figma | Design, Engineering | Design lead | 1 business day |
| Salesforce | Sales | Sales manager | 2 business days |
| VPN | All employees | IT Team | 1 business day |

## Rules
- AWS Console access requires that you have completed the security awareness training first.
- Access is granted by the approver, not by the person who raises the request.
- Requests need a short business reason, for example the project you will work on.
- If your role is not listed for a tool, ask your manager to raise the request for you.
