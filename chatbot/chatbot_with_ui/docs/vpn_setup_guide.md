# VPN Setup Guide
Last updated: 2026-03-05
Applies to: All employees
Owner: IT Team

## Who needs the VPN
Everyone who works outside the office network needs the VPN to reach internal tools such as the staging environment, the internal wiki, and the finance systems. Cloud tools such as Slack and Google Workspace do not need it.

## Getting access
VPN access is not enabled automatically. Raise an access request for VPN. IT approves it, usually within 1 business day.

## Setup steps
1. Install the Acme Secure Connect client from the Self Service app on your company laptop.
2. Sign in with your company email.
3. Approve the sign-in on your authenticator app (multi-factor authentication is mandatory).
4. Choose the "Acme-Global" profile and click Connect.

## Rules
- Do not install the VPN client on personal devices.
- Disconnect when you are not using internal tools.
- Public Wi-Fi is allowed only when the VPN is connected.

## Troubleshooting
If you cannot connect, restart the client and check that your authenticator app time is correct. Still stuck? Message #it-help on Slack or email it-help@acmedemo.example with a screenshot of the error.
