# Who To Contact For What
Last updated: 2026-03-01
Applies to: All employees
Owner: People Team

| Topic | Contact |
|---|---|
| Leave, benefits, policies, HR paperwork | people@acmedemo.example or #ask-hr |
| Laptop, VPN, accounts, tool access | it-help@acmedemo.example or #it-help |
| Payroll and salary credit date | payroll@acmedemo.example |
| Expense claims | finance@acmedemo.example |
| Lost device, phishing, security incident | security@acmedemo.example |
| Harassment, discrimination or ethics concerns | ethics@acmedemo.example (confidential) |

Sensitive matters such as pay discussions, visas, medical situations, or complaints should go to the People Team directly and not through chat tools.
